About Department

The Department of Information and Technology at CHARUSAT is committed to providing quality education, innovative research opportunities, and industry-oriented learning experiences. The department prepares students to become skilled professionals in emerging technologies.

  • Experienced Faculty Members
  • Modern Computer Laboratories
  • Industry Collaboration
  • Research and Innovation
  • Excellent Placement Support

Programs Offered

B.Tech Information and Technology

Undergraduate program focused on programming, software development, computer systems, and emerging technologies.

M.Tech Computer Engineering

Postgraduate program focused on advanced computing, research, and specialized technologies.

Ph.D. Program

Research-oriented program for advanced study and innovation in computer engineering.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Explore intelligent systems, machine learning, deep learning, and AI applications.

Cyber Security

Learn network security, ethical hacking, cryptography, and digital security.

Data Science

Develop skills in data analysis, visualization, statistics, and data-driven decision making.
